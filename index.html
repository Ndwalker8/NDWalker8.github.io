About

Aerospace and mechanical engineer with a focus on building things that survive extreme environments — whether that means hypersonic flow, turbine blades at temperature, or repaired structures under load.

I'm currently finishing my Master of Engineering at RPI, where my research focuses on cold spray additive manufacturing for structural repair. Before that, I spent a semester at GE Vernova working on steam turbine design, and I've been involved in research on structural health monitoring and damage diagnostics since my junior year.

Outside of the lab, I'm a member of the Rensselaer Rocket Society and the Spaceflight Society, and I've been part of Lambda Chi Alpha throughout my time at RPI. I also hold a minor in Economics.
