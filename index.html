About Me

Welcome to my Engineering Portfolio. My name is Nicholas Walker, and I'm an Aerospace and Mechanical Engineer with a strong background in CAD simulations and coding.

I’m pursuing a B.S. in Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI), graduating May 2025, with a minor in Economics.

I’ve gained hands-on experience through my roles at General Electric, contributing to steam turbine bucket simulations, and conducting research at RPI focused on FEM/SEM damage diagnostics.

My work includes UAV diagnostics, heat sink design, and flight dynamics. I'm proficient in CATIA, ANSYS, Siemens NX, MATLAB, Python, and more.

Thanks for visiting my portfolio — I’m excited to contribute to future engineering advancements!
